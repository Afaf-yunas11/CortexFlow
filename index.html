1. Opportunity Detection

Keyword and phrase signals identify scholarships, internships, fellowships, competitions, admissions, and research calls.

2. Structured Extraction

The parser pulls deadlines, minimum CGPA, location, required documents, benefits, and action links from messy email text.

3. Personalized Ranking

A deterministic scoring engine combines profile fit, urgency, support value, and evidence completeness into a priority score.

4. Agent-Ready Extensions

LangChain agents can later replace each stage, while a local Hugging Face model can strengthen classification and spam filtering.
